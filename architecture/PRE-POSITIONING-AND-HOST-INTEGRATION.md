## Bidirectional capability learning

The intended model is one logical PRE competency core consumed through the PRE Standalone adapter and A.S.F.R.E.E PRE adapter. Generic, host-neutral competencies may flow in either direction after provenance, sanitization, validation, compatibility and policy checks.

Classify each proposal as SHARED_CAPABILITY, HOST_ADAPTATION, HOST_SPECIFIC, or QUARANTINED_OR_REJECTED. Do not propagate credentials, private case artifacts, sensitive personal data, tenant-specific records or host-only permissions. Preserve origin lineage, digests, schema/package versions, idempotency keys, adoption receipts, migration and rollback. Authority/security/privacy conflicts must not use last-write-wins.
