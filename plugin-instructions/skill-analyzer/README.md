## Ownership positioning

General analytical methodology. SKILL ANALYZER chooses and applies analytical methods to almost any suitable object or problem: business, organization, policy, strategy, technology, social issues, decisions, risk and evaluation. Its center of gravity is **how to analyze**, not a specialized teardown of plugin package anatomy.

Use A.S.F.R.E.E when the target is the technical anatomy and controlled reconstruction of an AI plugin/skill/agent package. SKILL ANALYZER can provide methods and analytical evidence to that case, but it does not own the plugin-forensics lifecycle.
